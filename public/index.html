Controls

  • W, A, S, D: Move character
  • Shift: Run
  • Space: Jump
  • Mouse: Look around
  • E: Draw on walls (when prompt appears)
  • T: Toggle mouse positioning for drawing
  • Escape: Cancel drawing

Find a wall, look at it closely, and press E to draw! Move your mouse to position your drawing.

Your drawings will be visible to other players.
